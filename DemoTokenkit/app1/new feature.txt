Weekly Habits Track completions within each calendar week. A week meets its goal when the completion count reaches the configured weekly target. Calculate consistency based on qualifying weeks. Define how the current incomplete week is treated. Do not apply daily streak rules directly to weekly habits. General Rules Use the user's configured timezone or the existing application's timezone convention. Avoid UTC conversion errors when handling date-only values.
